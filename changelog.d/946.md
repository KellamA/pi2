### Added
- Agents can show you what they made: the new `present` tool puts up to six pictures or web pages (a mockup an agent wrote) side by side in a card in the conversation, labelled and captioned, and can ask which one you want, with a button for each and a field for your own words. A page is drawn on your Mac with no network, no files beyond its own and nothing it can open or ask, and the agent is told what it logged and what it was refused. Expand opens the presentation in a window of its own.
