### For contributors
- `pi2-layout-check` also checks a project hub's panes (`HubLayout` in PaneKit): the board once at most and always within reach, where an opened conversation goes, the ready-made layouts, the least size every pane keeps, and damaged saved panes. `pi2-window-check` shows the hub with two and three panes beside the conversation at three widths. `scripts/test-copy show <suffix> beside:<id>` opens one of a project's conversations in a new pane, as ⌥ does. The split view, its dividers and the click watch the old screens' panes used moved from `Workspace.swift` to `Panes.swift`, which outlasts it.
