### For contributors
- Projects (preview, engine only): a project's orchestrator can change its project with `project_agents`, `project_settings` and `project_rules`. Each change is recorded in the record of changes (`pi2.changes`) as a typed operation with its own Undo, or none, shown in the conversation's state as `projectChanges` and undone with `changes.undo`; `changes.list` lists them. A change that widens what agents may do is never made by the tool: it is put under Needs you as a request, checked again when the person allows it. Notes added and removed with `remember` and `forget` in a project are recorded too. See docs/projects.md, 3.12, 5.2 to 5.4 and section 7.
