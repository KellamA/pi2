### Fixed
- With many agents working, pi² kept its own main thread busy most of the time, so clicks, menus and opening a conversation waited behind it. Each spinner in the sidebar laid the window out again many times a second, and an update to any conversation looked at since pi² started drew the toolbar, the menus and every pane again. Spinners are now drawn without the app's help, an update draws again only what shows it, and a conversation no pane has shown for two minutes stops being sent to the app.
- A conversation that is loading says so in its pane, instead of an empty pane with "Model" and "Tools off" in the toolbar as if nothing were chosen.
- A message sent while pi² or its engine is busy shows at once by the message field, as sending and then sent, until the conversation shows it. If the engine stops before it has said whether it took the message, pi² sends it again once the engine is back, in a way the engine recognises, so it is neither lost nor taken twice.
