### Added
- With projects on, a conversation in a project has a menu of its own again: right-click a working agent's row or one of the project's other conversations in the sidebar, or open the ⋯ menu in the header of an agent's conversation beside the orchestrator, to stop it while it works, rename it, export it as Markdown or JSON, archive it or delete it (after the same confirmation as before). Archiving or deleting the conversation shown beside the orchestrator brings the board back. The project's own row can export the conversation with the orchestrator, and Conversation › Rename…, Archive and Delete say why they are greyed for it.
- A sub-chat shown beside the orchestrator has a Parent button that goes back to the conversation that handed it its work.
- The hub's bar has a Thinking menu beside the orchestrator's model, and the ⋯ menu of an agent's conversation has the agent's model and Thinking. In a project the model and thinking belong to the agent: changing them changes all of that agent's conversations from their next message.
