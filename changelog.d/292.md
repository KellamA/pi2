### Added
- Settings › Providers has a section for models on this Mac. It downloads the Strata engine and a Qwen3.8-Flash-Next pack, showing each one's licence first, its size, and for each pack the memory it needs, what is lost against the others, whether it fits this Mac, and which one pi² recommends. Downloads can be paused, resumed and cancelled, carry on after pi² quits or the network drops, check the free disk space first, and are kept out of backups. pi² sets up nothing whose checksum does not match the one it knows for that file. A model's context size, pictures and requests at once can be set, with what each costs in memory.
