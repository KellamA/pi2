### Changed
- A run waiting for a subscription's limit no longer looks like one that is working. Its row in the sidebar, its pane and Branches show an hourglass and the time it carries on, Activity counts it apart ("11 running, 8 waiting for a limit") and names whose limit it waits for, the menu bar says so, and the conversation that handed out the work has one line saying how many of its sub-chats wait, for which provider and until when.
- When a provider's limit is reached, and again when the work that waited carries on, the Inbox says so, in the same notice that warned of the window at 90%, so the three read as one story. Each of the two also posts a notification while pi² is in the background; Settings › General has a switch to turn them off.
- `sub_chats`' list says which sub-chats are waiting for a provider's limit and until when, so an agent that hands out work can give new work to an agent on another subscription.
