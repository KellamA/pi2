### Fixed
- With a model that does not take a system message mid-conversation (an endpoint of your own such as llama.cpp, LM Studio, Ollama or vLLM; DeepSeek on OpenRouter; GLM and DeepSeek on OpenCode Go; most of OpenRouter's models), every change to the system prompt, such as a background command starting or ending, changed the start of the prompt, so the provider's prompt cache kept nothing and the whole conversation was read, and paid for, as new. Now pi² sends such a change where it happened, as a note in the conversation marked as pi²'s and not the person's, and the start of the prompt stays the same. Models that take a later system message (Anthropic's, Codex, the models on this Mac) are sent it as before.
