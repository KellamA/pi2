### Added
- With Projects (preview) on, a working row on a project's Now tab or in a Dashboard column has Stop, shown on hover or when the keyboard reaches it, and in the row's menu. The Dashboard has Stop All at the end of its line across all projects (and ⌥⌘. in its View menu), and the palette has Stop All Runs; both ask first, naming how many runs they stop.
- With Projects (preview) on, a working row shows the shell command its agent waits on, how long it has run and how long it has printed nothing, as Activity does, so a stuck agent can be told from a slow one.
- With Projects (preview) on, the Dashboard shows a subscription's limit notices under its line across all projects, with Dismiss and Show Usage, and the notice that the engine restarted after stopping unexpectedly as a banner over the Dashboard or a project's hub, with what was picked up and what was cut off.
