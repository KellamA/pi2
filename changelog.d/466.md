### For contributors
- Projects (preview, #466): a project's orchestrator is told what changed in the project at the start of each turn, in a catch-up in front of its input, and how the project stands at the start of a new conversation and of each summary, as entries of its transcript rather than a prompt section. `projects.startFresh` and `projects.setOrchestrator` begin a fresh conversation with it, moving what the old one was waiting for; a message the person sends another agent of the project is told to the orchestrator at its next turn. Every conversation of a project has a `project` section. Engine only: nothing a person sees changes, and a conversation in no project is told exactly what it was. pi durable is patched to let the engine add entries as an input is placed (`engine/scripts/patch-inputs.ts`).
