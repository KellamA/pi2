### Fixed
- pi² was slow to open a conversation and to take a message while many agents worked. With the login item on, the engine ran as background work, at the lowest priority macOS has and on the efficiency cores alone, and it held its one thread each time it started a Keychain or git process, which it did for every model request and every few seconds for every listed folder. The login item now runs the engine as an app's own processes run (an existing one is put right when this update replaces the engine), sign-ins read from the Keychain are kept, and those processes are started off the engine's thread. Measured with 20 agents at work on a Mac at a load of 90: opening a conversation and sending a message took 15 and 30 ms at the worst, against up to 1.5 s before, with stalls of a second or more.
- A message pi² had taken could come back as refused, and be put back in the box, when something it does after taking it failed; sending it again sent it twice.
