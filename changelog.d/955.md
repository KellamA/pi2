### Fixed
- Computer use: a click or a context menu at a point in a throwaway test copy, approved by a card, could go ahead after the copy's window had changed since the agent last looked, such as a tab switched away, because the control the card named went on answering at its old place. Now that control must still be among the window's controls, or the action is refused and the agent is told to look again. If it does not answer through accessibility, no real click is sent to the point in its place.
