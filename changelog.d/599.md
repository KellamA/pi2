### Changed
- Projects (preview): a project's Work tab opens at once on the list as it was last read, and reads GitHub behind it, with a small spinner by "read … ago" while it does; the rows change in place when the read lands. Reading a repository asks GitHub its questions together, and only for what changed since the last read, and leaves what is closed brief until it is opened: on a repository with hundreds of issues closed this week, a read that took about twenty seconds takes one or two. Opening an issue or a pull request shows what the list knows at once and fills in its text, comments and checks as they arrive, and an action shows at once as on its way, then settles, or is put back with GitHub's words.
- Projects (preview): the Work tab is one calm page, as Notes and Rules are: one line a row, title first, with its number, who has it and when it changed; pull requests in a section of their own above the issues, with their checks and review as small marks; and an opened issue or pull request with its title, one line of where it is, its actions in a bar that stays put, its text drawn as the conversation draws Markdown, and its comments with who wrote them and when.
- Projects (preview): each section of the Work tab's list (Pull requests, Open, In progress, Waiting, Blocked, Done, Dropped) folds and unfolds with a click on its heading, or with the keyboard, and keeps its count while folded. Each project remembers which are folded, after quitting too. Done starts folded, and so do the pull requests merged or closed; the others start open.
