### For contributors
- The app carries a helper, `Contents/MacOS/pi2-peer` (`app/Sources/pi2-peer`, PeerKit), which the engine asks whether a connection is the app's; `build.sh` builds, copies and signs it, `pi2-peer-check` checks it in CI, and `check-release-build.mts` checks that a release has it and that the engine takes answers from the app and refuses its own connection. A development engine knows its app from `PI2_APP_BUNDLE`, which the app sets.
- `scripts/test-copy answer` and `call` give the copy's engine a key it writes at each start into `app/build/test-copies/<suffix>/answer-key`, with which they may answer for the person; a release engine has neither. The engine's tests that answer start their engines with a stand-in app bundle (`standInApp` in `engine/test/patience.ts`), and `engine/test/answers.ts` checks the refusals.
