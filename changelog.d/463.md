### For contributors
- Projects (preview, engine only): each run a project's orchestrator hands to a sub-chat has a report, given to it once, by waking it, by a wait or by its new `reports` tool. Whether a report wakes it is the project's switch, or the orchestrator's choice per hand-off (each, a batch once all of it has ended, only on a failure, or not at all), or the person's (`reports.wake`). Outside a project, and with the preview off, waking is as before. See `docs/wakes.md` and `docs/projects.md` (#463).
