### For contributors
- `scripts/auto-merge` no longer takes an error of `gh pr checks` (GitHub not answering, say) for "no required check has reported yet" and goes on waiting: it shows gh's error and exits 1. A required check that failed, timed out or was cancelled is named at once with its link, even while others still run, with the commands to read its log or run it again; one no runner took at the run's fourth attempt, which CI no longer runs again by itself, is left to you rather than waited for. Its test fakes gh's answers for each case, a check that is not required failing among them.
- The workers' brief (`docs/agents.md`) and Testing safely say to take a picture of a throwaway copy's own window only, never of a region of the screen or the whole screen, which shows what the person has open and can ask for Screen Recording.
