### Added
- While any conversation is working, or waiting for a provider's limit or the network to carry on by itself, pi² now keeps this Mac from going to sleep by itself (the display can still sleep). The switch is in Settings › General, on by default, with "Also on battery" under it; the sidebar's "Engine running" line says when the Mac is being kept awake. Closing the lid of a Mac with no display attached still puts it to sleep, which no app can prevent.
