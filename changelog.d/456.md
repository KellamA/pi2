### For contributors
- The engine knows projects (#456, the first step of the projects epic, [docs/projects.md](docs/projects.md)), behind a preview switch that nothing in the app turns on yet: `projects.setPreview { on: true }` through a throwaway copy's engine (`scripts/test-copy call`) does. With it on, `projects.create` makes a project from a folder in one step, with its orchestrator and an empty conversation with it, and `projects.rename`, `projects.set`, `projects.archive`, `projects.unarchive`, `projects.delete` and `projects.list` work, with a `projects` event for the apps. With the preview off, every request that exists today works as before and a conversation in no project is told exactly what it was told.
  `engine/src/projects.ts` holds what the later issues of the epic build on (`projectsOn`, `inProject`, `requireSwitch`, `addNews`, the `admit` stub) and the places they fill in. The demo model answers `/system full` with the whole prompt and every tool declaration, for tests that compare them.
