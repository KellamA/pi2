### Changed
- With Projects (preview) on, Activity and Usage are rows under Shared in the sidebar, beside Templates and the Archive. Activity now lists only what keeps running apart from the agents: background commands left running, with Output, Open and Stop, and models running on this Mac. The agents' runs, the restart notice and schedules are on Now, the Dashboard and each project's Settings tab. Usage's row says "over budget" as before, and Usage shows today's spend by project. The Dashboard's "today" opens Usage.
- With Projects (preview) on, the Inbox is no longer offered (View › Show Inbox, ⇧⌘I and the palette's Go to Inbox): Now, the Dashboard and the menu bar item show what waits. A project's Branches tab has Refresh.
