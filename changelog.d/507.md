### Added
- Projects (preview): a worker, a sub-chat of a project's agent, can now put a question or a decision to you under Needs you and carry on working, instead of waiting for your answer. The card says which agent asks, in which sub-chat, and on which piece of work; the sub-chat's name opens it. Your answer reaches the worker while it is still working; once it has finished, the answer goes to the agent that handed it the work, which is told whose question it was and how to continue the sub-chat. A question the worker cannot work on without still waits for you, as before.
