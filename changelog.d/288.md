### Added
- Settings › Chat: instructions sent to every chat (what chats should know about you and how to answer), the model a new chat starts with, whether chats read the web, and chat mode's memory.
- Chat mode remembers lasting things you tell it, across chats, in notes of its own that you can see, edit, delete and add to in Settings › Chat, with a switch to turn memory off. A chat can also read what your agents remember, when a question calls for it, but never change it; it does not work the other way, and no agent sees what chats remember. Settings › Chat lists the agents' notes too, read-only.
