### For contributors
- pi² Remote, the helper the engine starts for a paired iPhone (docs/pi2-remote.md), is built into the app as `Contents/Helpers/pi² Remote.app`. A development build's runs in loopback mode only and cannot ask macOS for anything; `pi2-remote-helper-check` checks it in CI, with a real engine and a phone pairing and attaching over 127.0.0.1. `scripts/check-protected-targets` now also holds pi² Remote's targets, the Swift tools version and the package's language mode, and refuses `@testable import` of RemoteKit or pi² Remote.
