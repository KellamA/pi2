### Fixed
- Computer use: an action on a control by its number (click, context menu, type, scroll) could act on a control the app had since taken away, such as one on a tab switched away, which goes on answering at its old place and can still be pressed. So could a click or context menu at a point in a throwaway test copy with no approval card. Now the control must still be among the window's controls, or nothing is done and the agent is told to look again. A real click at a control's centre goes where the control is now.
