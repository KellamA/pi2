### Changed
- With the Projects preview on, Quick Ask (⌃⌥Space) and the menu bar's ask field start a chat, on the chat model used last, and Open in pi² shows it in chat mode. Before, they started a conversation with an agent template, which a project does not use.
- With the Projects preview on, Continue with an Agent in chat mode asks for a project (with New Project… at the foot of the list) and, if you like, one of its agents, in place of an agent and a folder. To the orchestrator, the chat goes into its conversation as your message, and the project's hub shows it; to another agent, it starts a new conversation in the project, shown in place of the board. With the preview off, both work as before.
