### Changed
- Projects (preview): each column of the Dashboard now shows a small window onto the conversation with the project's orchestrator, live: its last few turns, with each run of tool calls as one quiet line, over the field that messages it, so a short exchange needs no trip to the hub. The window can be made taller by dragging its top edge or folded away, for each column, and a click on its header opens the hub. Under each column's header, one line says what needs you, who is working (and who waits for a provider's limit, until when), what finished today and how much of it the orchestrator has not read, and what the project spent today against its limit; each part opens the hub where it is shown.
- Projects (preview): a Dashboard column also shows what finished today, folded with its count and how many reports the orchestrator has not read, and, for a project whose work is a GitHub repository, the pull requests waiting to be merged, each with its checks and review in a word; a click opens one on the Work tab. A line across the top counts what needs you in all (⌥⌘0 brings the first into view), who is working and what was spent today, with each subscription's windows and a mark while a provider refuses for its limit. Columns can be put in your own order by dragging their headers, or folded to a narrow strip, and ⌥⌘1 to ⌥⌘9 put the keyboard in a column's field.
