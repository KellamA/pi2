### Added
- **Chat mode, beside the agent view.** A switch at the top of the sidebar, and Agents (⌘1) and Chat (⌘2) in the View menu, move the window between your agents and a place to ask questions. The window opens on the side it was last on, and each side keeps what it showed while the other is on screen; while you are in chat mode the switch shows what is waiting on you and whether agents are working. Chat mode's sidebar has New Chat (⌘N there), a search of the chats' titles and messages, and the chats by date, pinned ones first; a chat can be renamed, pinned, archived or deleted from its context menu. A chat is one centred column with the conversation page agents use, the model and thinking effort in the toolbar as for an agent's conversation, and Stop while an answer is written; an empty one shows the message field in the middle with a few ways to begin. Chats are found by the command palette too, marked as chats, and are not listed among agents' conversations.
