### Added
- A model downloaded in Settings › Providers › On this Mac can now be used. Once its files have been checked, pi² prepares it for Strata with Strata's own setup, which takes a few minutes and fetches 5.2 GB more for the draft layer that makes answers faster; the model's row shows each step, Pause and Resume carry on where it stopped, and a preparation that fails says why, with its log and Try Again. Only then is the model offered. A model whose files were downloaded with 0.8.0 is prepared by itself once the engine is set up.
- If you already have a model's files on this Mac, from Strata or a download of your own, **Agree and Use Files on This Mac…** in the download sheet takes them from a folder you choose instead of downloading them again: each is cloned (taking no room on the same disk) or copied, and used only if its checksum matches; the rest is downloaded. A draft layer Strata fetched before is taken from there too.
- The download sheet shows the part of the model's licence you should read before agreeing in full: the Qwen Community License's second clause, which asks a business that offers models as a service, or an AI coding or office assistant, to get a separate licence for commercial use.
