### Changed
- An agent's `sub_chats` list no longer names every archived sub-chat and what each used, which in a project's orchestrator grew into thousands of tokens read again at every later turn. It lists those that are not archived, then counts the archived in one line; `archived: true` pages through them, newest first, `usage: true` adds what each used, and `chats` looks one up by its number.
- The `work` tool's actions other than `list` and `show` answer in a line saying what was done and to which item, with a new issue's page on GitHub, instead of the whole item with its text and every note.
