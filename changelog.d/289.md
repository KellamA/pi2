### Added
- **Continue with an Agent…** hands a chat to one of your agents, in a folder you choose, when it turns into real work: from the chat's toolbar, its context menu, the Conversation menu or the command palette. The agent gets the chat as it stands, with its pictures and files, as the first message of a new conversation in the agent view, and can be told what to do next. It works with its own access and approvals; nothing in the chat counts as an approval, and nothing chat mode remembers is passed on. The chat stays as it was, and each links to the other.
