### Added
- With projects on, the hub's conversation panels have the context meter the old screens' toolbar has: in the header of the conversation with the orchestrator, and of an agent's conversation shown in place of the board. It shows how full the model's context is, what the last request read and what the conversation has used, and offers Summarise Earlier Messages, Summarise With a Note…, and for the orchestrator Start Fresh…, which begins a new conversation with the orchestrator after saying what it starts with (the project as it stands, its rules and notes) and that the present one is kept to read. A fresh conversation and the one it followed link to each other, and open in the hub.
- A project's Settings tab sets the size at which the orchestrator's conversation is summarised, beside its model.
