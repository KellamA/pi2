### For contributors
- The relay for pi² for iPhone has its two deployments, `development` and `production`, in `relay/wrangler.jsonc`, and a workflow, `.github/workflows/relay.yml`, that deploys one only when the owner starts it on `main` and approves it in that relay's protected environment. `relay/scripts/deploy.mjs` checks a deployment's configuration before it deploys (logs off, no preview addresses, no secret among the variables), and `npm run deploy -- <relay> --dry-run` builds it without uploading anything, as CI now does on every pull request. `relay/scripts/register.mjs` registers a test pairing at a deployed relay. `docs/relay.md` has the owner's steps, the spending limit and how to turn the relay off for everyone; `SECURITY.md` covers the iPhone app, pi² Remote and the relay. Nothing has been deployed.
