### Added
- With projects on, the place beside the conversation with the orchestrator splits into panes, so two or three agents of a project can be watched and written to at once. View › Split Right (⌘D) and Split Down (⇧⌘D) add a pane that offers the project's working agents first, then its other conversations, to the keyboard; Close Pane (⌃⌘W), Next and Previous Pane (⌘] and ⌘[, the orchestrator's conversation among them), Even Out and the ready-made layouts work on them. Each pane shows the board or one conversation, with the agent's header and a close button; the board shows once at most, and closing the last conversation brings it back.
- A conversation opened from the sidebar, Now, the Work tab or a link goes to the focused pane, and with ⌥ held, a sub-chat card's Beside or the sidebar's Open in New Pane, to a new pane beside it. A conversation dragged from the sidebar shows in the pane it is dropped on, or in a new one when dropped on a pane's right or bottom edge.
- Each project keeps its panes and their sizes, and comes back to them; a pane whose conversation is archived or deleted closes. Panes never get narrower than 320 points: a window too narrow for the conversation beside them folds it, as it folds the board, and a split that cannot fit is refused with a line that says why. In a narrow pane the message field shows the agent's avatar without its name, and Stop without its word.
