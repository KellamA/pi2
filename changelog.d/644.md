### Changed
- With Projects (preview) on, every way to open a conversation now opens it where it lives, never in the old panes: the palette's results and messages found, the Archive's Open, Activity, the Branches tab, the menu bar item, Quick Ask's Open in pi², a background command's source, a local model's Open, a need's card, Continue with an Agent, a sub-chat's card, Fork From Here and ⌘N. The orchestrator's conversation opens in its project's hub; any other conversation of a project, archived ones and sub-chats included, opens in place of the project's board; a chat in chat mode; and a conversation of a deleted or archived project, or of none, in the Archive, which now reads it in place of its list. With nothing chosen, and at launch, the window shows the Dashboard (or the hub of your one project, or, with none, a page to make the first) instead of the old Welcome page.
