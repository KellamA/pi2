What the code holdsThe Mac's name, how to reach it on this network, the fingerprint of its key, and a one-time secret. No account, no password.
Why digits as wellThe code could be photographed from across a room. The digits are worked out from the encrypted connection itself, so a second phone cannot pair in the person's place unnoticed.
After pairingThe phone asks for notifications, then Face ID, each with a line on what it is for. Either can be refused; Face ID's answers then stay on the Mac.

The phone opens on one list. What needs the person first, answered in place; then the Dashboard and the projects, with the badges of the Mac's sidebar; then chats and the archive. The bar on top says which Mac and how it is reached.
